---
title: Multi-currency
description: Price in one currency, settle in whatever the payer used.
icon: Globe2
---

You can price a payment in **any active currency** — `XAF`, `USD`, `NGN`, `BTC`, and more.
There's no account-level "settlement currency" to match — every payment settles
independently, in whatever currency your customer actually pays with.

```bash
curl -X POST https://connect.tchokopay.com/v1/collect/checkout \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer tchoko_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 50,
    "currency": "USD",
    "description": "Premium plan — 1 month"
  }'
```

Before anyone has paid, the response mirrors exactly what you asked for — nothing is
converted yet:

```json
{
  "reference": "REQ-1785296850838-A5303B",
  "status": "PENDING",
  "amount": 50,
  "currency": "USD",
  "requestedAmount": 50,
  "requestedCurrency": "USD",
  "checkoutUrl": "https://tchokopay.com/pay/REQ-1785296850838-A5303B"
}
```

## After payment, `amount`/`currency` reflect what actually settled

Say your customer pays with Bitcoin. Once payment completes,
`GET /v1/collect/:reference` returns:

```json
{
  "reference": "REQ-1785296850838-A5303B",
  "status": "SUCCESS",
  "amount": 0.00078347,
  "currency": "BTC",
  "requestedAmount": 50,
  "requestedCurrency": "USD"
}
```

You settle net of fees in **whatever currency your customer actually paid with** — your
wallet is genuinely multi-currency, with one real balance per currency that's ever
come in. A customer paying in XAF, another in NGN, and another in BTC for the same
`$50` price point all land in three separate wallet balances — there's currently no
automatic conversion or consolidation into a single currency.

<Callout type="info">
  `requestedAmount`/`requestedCurrency` never change once a payment is created.
  `amount`/`currency` always reflect the live/settled truth — identical to the
  request before payment, the real settled amount after.
</Callout>

## Where the money goes

Money accepted through the API stays on TchokoPay — held in your wallet, not routed
anywhere automatically — until you withdraw it from your merchant dashboard
(`/merchant/payouts`). There's no per-payment payout dispatch to configure or think
about; every payment, in every currency, settles the same way.
