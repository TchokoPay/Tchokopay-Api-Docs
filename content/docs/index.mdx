TchokoPay's Merchant API moves money in both directions from your own backend. You build
the product; we are the rails underneath it.

**Take payments** — create a charge, send your customer to a hosted checkout page carrying
your branding, and get notified the moment it resolves. No payment UI to build, no mobile
money integration to maintain.

**Send payouts** — move money out to any mobile money number, from your own systems.
Whoever your business needs to pay, and however many of them.

Both halves are built to be embedded in something else. An e-commerce platform settling
its sellers, a payments app moving money for its own users, a fintech that would rather
partner than negotiate with every operator itself — the API does not care what you are,
and imposes no shape on your product.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create a payment

`POST /v1/payments` with an amount, a currency, and a description. You get back a
`checkoutUrl`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Redirect your customer

Send them to `checkoutUrl`. They pick MTN MoMo, Orange Money, card, or Bitcoin/Lightning
(where available) and complete the payment on TchokoPay's own hosted page — you never
touch payment details.
</Step>
<Step>
### Get notified

Either poll `GET /v1/payments/:reference`, or register a webhook once and get a signed
`POST` the moment the payment succeeds or fails.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- **Multi-currency pricing** — price in any currency, settle net of fees in whatever your
  customer actually paid with. See [Multi-currency](/docs/guides/multi-currency).
- **Signed, replay-protected webhooks** — HMAC-signed, timestamp-bound, automatically
  retried with backoff. See [Webhooks](/docs/guides/webhooks).
- **Idempotent requests** — retry any `POST /v1/payments` call safely with an
  `Idempotency-Key`. See [Idempotency & retries](/docs/guides/idempotency).
- **Key-scoped access** — generate, rotate, and revoke API keys yourself from your
  merchant dashboard. Revocation is immediate.

## Base URL

```
https://connect.tchokopay.com
```

This is the *only* domain the Merchant API is reachable from — it exposes nothing beyond
the routes documented here.

## Rollout

Cameroon (MTN MoMo, Orange Money) at launch, with more countries opening over time. Check
[`GET /v1/channels`](/docs/api-reference/list-channels) to see what's live for your
account right now.
