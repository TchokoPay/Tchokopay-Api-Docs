The TchokoPay API moves money in both directions. You call it from your backend; we
handle the mobile money networks.

## Two ways to collect

Both settle into the same balance and report through the same webhook. Pick per payment —
you can use both.

| | Charge a number | Hosted checkout |
|---|---|---|
| | [`POST /v1/collect/charge`](/docs/api-reference/charge) | [`POST /v1/collect/checkout`](/docs/api-reference/create-checkout) |
| Customer experience | Stays in your app. A prompt arrives on their phone. | Redirected to a TchokoPay page, then back. |
| You collect | Their number and network | Nothing |
| You build | Your own payment form | A redirect |
| Available methods | Mobile money | Mobile money, card, Bitcoin, Lightning |

If you already have the customer's phone number, charge it. If you'd rather not handle
numbers or network selection, redirect.

## Sending money

[`POST /v1/disburse`](/docs/api-reference/create-disbursement) sends money from your
TchokoPay balance to a mobile money number. You name the amount, the country, the network
and the number; we deliver it and tell you when it lands.

Your balance is funded by what you collect. Disbursement is enabled per account — ask us
if it isn't on yours yet.

## Base URL

```
https://connect.tchokopay.com
```

Every endpoint is under `/v1`. All requests need an `Authorization: Bearer` header.

## Where we operate

Cameroon (MTN MoMo, Orange Money) today, with more countries opening over time.

Rather than hardcoding that, call [`GET /v1/countries`](/docs/api-reference/countries) and
[`GET /v1/account/providers`](/docs/api-reference/account-providers) — they tell you what
is available to your account right now, and stay correct as we expand.
